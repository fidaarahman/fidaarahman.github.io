This paper introduces an integrated multi-modal artificial intelligence framework for credit card fraud detection. The approach combines Transformer architectures with Graph Neural Networks (GNN), achieving a recall of 99.2% and an AUC of 0.996 on benchmark datasets. The framework incorporates interpretable feature fusion and privacy-preserving AI components for secure and transparent financial transaction analysis.

**Published in:** Proceedings of the International Conference on Recent and Innovative Results in Engineering and Technology (ICRIRET 2026), Konya, Turkey.
