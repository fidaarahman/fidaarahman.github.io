An Integrated Multi-Modal AI Framework for Credit Card Fraud Detection

Status: Published — ICRIRET 2026 (Konya, Turkey)

Multi-modal AI framework combining Transformers and Graph Neural Networks for fraud detection.

This research paper presents an integrated multi-modal artificial intelligence framework designed for credit card fraud detection. The approach combines Transformer architectures with Graph Neural Networks to achieve robust fraud detection while maintaining high recall and precision.

Key contributions: The framework leverages multiple data modalities and achieves state-of-the-art performance with privacy-preserving AI integration for secure financial transactions.

Published in the proceedings of the International Conference on Recent and Innovative Results in Engineering and Technology (ICRIRET 2026), Konya, Turkey.
